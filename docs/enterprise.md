# The full edition

The full edition is the platform of this repository with modules that the open build does not hold.
It adds these parts, and nothing else:

- **Validation and regression.** Validation of reports against expected cases, on demand or on a
  schedule, regression runs, and replay from a validation case.
- **Document intake screening.** Screening of each upload before a run reads it.
- **Anonymisation of confidential runs.** The documents of a confidential run are anonymised before
  any model reads them.
- **Industry modules.** Hedge fund monitoring, private equity monitoring, and the Financial vertical
  with the Json2Xlsx block.
- **Two more blocks.** Peer Financials and Verification Group.
- **The Aspose document implementation.** Uploads of email files (.eml and .msg), with their
  attachments, and of Excel 97-2003 workbooks (.xls).
- **The library of tuned workflow templates.**

[The open build and the full edition](editions.md) compares the two builds area by area.

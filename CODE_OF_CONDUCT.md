# Code of conduct

This project welcomes everyone who wants to use it or improve it. Its issues, pull requests and
reviews stay about the work.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, in every comment and review.
- Criticize the work, not the person. Say what is wrong and how it could be better.
- Assume good faith. Ask before you conclude.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change that does not fit the project.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminating language or content about anyone's identity or background.
- Publishing another person's private information without their consent.
- Repeatedly pushing a discussion after a maintainer has closed it.

## What happens

The maintainers may edit or remove a comment, close an issue or pull request, or block a person
who breaks this code. To report a problem, follow the private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md),
and say that it is a conduct report. The maintainers read every report and keep the reporter's
identity private.
